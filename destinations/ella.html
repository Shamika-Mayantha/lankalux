Ella

Mist, tea, and a village that works best when you are not racing the clock.

Why visit

Ella sits in the hill country with views over tea and forested slopes. It is a natural pause after Kandy or Nuwara Eliya, and a centrepiece of the Hill Country and Scenic Rail Journey.

The train

Many travellers reach Ella by the hill-country train, one of the island's slow pleasures. Seats are arranged in advance where we can; tickets are a separate cost. Your chauffeur-guide typically meets you at the station. The train, explained covers what to expect.

How many nights

Two nights lets you walk a viewpoint at a civilised hour and keep an unhurried afternoon. Little Adam's Peak and Nine Arches are popular; we would rather you do one well than three in a rush.

Onward

From Ella you can descend toward Yala or the south coast. A tailor-made plan can linger in tea country if that is the point of the trip.
