A Few Practical Answers

What is included in a LankaLux journey?

A private vehicle for your stay, airport pickup and drop-off, an English-speaking chauffeur-guide, and a custom itinerary. Hotels can be yours, arranged by LankaLux, or a mix of both. We plan the days around you rather than a fixed group package.

Is the chauffeur also a guide?

Yes. You travel with a dedicated private chauffeur-guide: one person who drives, helps with pacing, and shares local context. We do not describe this as a licensed national-guide qualification.

Are safari jeeps, trains and entrance fees included?

Train tickets, safari jeep excursions, attraction entrance fees and optional activities are not included unless we specifically say so. We can arrange them where possible, with applicable fees paid separately or on site. For a limited time, confirmed bookings include a free safari jeep at one selected park (jeep only, not park tickets), until the end of October.

How do we start planning?

Share dates, who is travelling, and the kind of pace you like. We send a custom itinerary, then refine it with you. You can write to us, use WhatsApp, or begin from one of our journey ideas.

Can we travel with children?

Yes. We pace the days more gently, choose suitable vehicles, and keep room for rest. See Sri Lanka family tours for how we usually approach it.

Are the journeys on the website fixed packages?

No. They are starting points. Choose a journey you like and we will reshape it around you.

Which vehicles can we travel in?

We match the vehicle to your group, luggage and route. Options include a Toyota Voxy, Toyota KDH vans and a sedan. See Our Fleet.

Can you review an itinerary we already have?

Yes. Send your current hotels or route and we will look at pacing, driving times, stops and activities, and tell you what we would change. There is no obligation. Start from Already planning Sri Lanka?
