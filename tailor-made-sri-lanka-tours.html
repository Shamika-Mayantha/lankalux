Tailor-Made Sri Lanka Tours

A route written around your dates, your pace, and the kind of days you actually enjoy.

Not a standard package

LankaLux does not sell a locked coach tour. We start with how you like to travel: slow mornings, wildlife before breakfast, a train through tea country, or more time by the water. The itinerary is written for you, then adjusted as the trip unfolds.

Our journey ideas are starting points, not cages. The Signature Sri Lanka Journey is a balanced first visit. Wildlife, hills or coast can lead instead.

How planning works

You share dates, who is coming, and a few preferences. We return a custom itinerary with a private chauffeur-guide, a vehicle for the stay, airport pickup and drop-off, and handpicked hotels or villas. Then we refine it together before you fly.

Private from arrival to departure

You are not waiting for a group. A chauffeur-guide stays with you, which makes last-minute changes simple: an extra hour at a temple, a beach afternoon instead of another drive.

Fees to expect beyond the journey plan

Train tickets, safari jeeps, park tickets and optional activities are arranged where possible but are not included unless stated. A current booking offer includes a free safari jeep at one selected park (jeep only) until the end of October.

Families may prefer a gentler version: Sri Lanka family tours. First-timers often start with our 10-day itinerary.
