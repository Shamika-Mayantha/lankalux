A 10-Day Sri Lanka Itinerary

Enough days to see the shape of the island without treating it like a checklist.

Who this outline is for

Ten days is a sensible first visit: Cultural Triangle, Kandy and tea country, a wildlife park, then the south coast. LankaLux runs this as the Signature Sri Lanka Journey, always adjustable.

A calm ten-day shape

Arrive and sleep near Colombo. Move inland to Sigiriya and the ancient cities. Continue to Kandy, then into tea country toward Nuwara Eliya and Ella, often with a stretch by train. Descend to Yala for a safari, then the coast around Galle and Mirissa before you fly.

That is a sketch, not a prison timetable. A private chauffeur-guide makes the joins between regions human instead of heroic.

If you have fewer or more days

With a week, drop a region rather than rushing all of them. With two weeks, add a second park or more idle beach time. How many days do you need? is the fuller version of that advice.
